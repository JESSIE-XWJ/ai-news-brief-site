Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI News Brief require an account?

No. AI News Brief does not require an account or login.

Does the app collect my personal information?

The app does not require or directly collect common personal information such as name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, or financial information.

How do I save an article?

Open an article and use the save/bookmark control. Saved article IDs are stored locally on your device.

How do I remove a saved article?

Use the save/bookmark control again to remove it from your saved list.

How do I clear local app data?

Uninstall the app to remove its locally stored data.

How does Daily Brief work?

Daily Brief uses local iOS notifications scheduled on your device.

Can I read the original article?

Yes. Select “Read original” on an article page. The original article opens in your device’s browser.
