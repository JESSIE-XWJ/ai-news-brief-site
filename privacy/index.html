Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

AI News Brief is an iOS application that provides concise summaries of AI and technology news.

1. Information We Collect

AI News Brief does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

The app does not directly collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Health information
  • Financial information

The app does not use advertising or analytics services.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

AI News Brief stores a small amount of information locally on your device to support app features:

  • Saved Articles — the identifiers of articles you save (ai-brief.saved-ids)
  • Daily Brief — your Daily Brief preference and notification time (ai-brief.daily-brief)
  • Appearance — your light or dark appearance preference (ai-brief.appearance)

This information is stored locally on your device and is not associated with a user account.

3. Notifications

AI News Brief uses local notifications for the Daily Brief feature. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app does not use remote push notifications for this feature.

4. Supabase and App Data

AI News Brief uses Supabase as backend infrastructure to provide published news content. The app primarily reads published article data from Supabase.

The app does not send saved article IDs, appearance preferences, Daily Brief settings, or user profiles to Supabase.

Supabase may process technical information and server logs as part of providing its infrastructure and services, subject to its own policies and practices.

5. AI Processing

AI News Brief uses DeepSeek in its server-side news processing pipeline. DeepSeek is used to process news content and generate summaries, why-it-matters explanations, key takeaways, and other AI-assisted editorial information.

The iOS app does not directly call the DeepSeek API. The server-side AI processing is designed to process news content rather than user profile information.

6. Third-Party News Websites

The app provides links to original news articles. When a user selects “Read original”, the original URL is opened using the device’s browser.

After leaving AI News Brief, the user is interacting with a third-party website. Third-party websites have their own privacy policies and terms. AI News Brief does not control the privacy practices of those websites.

7. How Information Is Used

Information handled by AI News Brief is used to:

  • Provide saved article functionality
  • Remember Daily Brief settings
  • Remember appearance preferences
  • Provide published news content
  • Generate AI-assisted news summaries
  • Provide links to original articles

AI News Brief does not use advertising, behavioral profiling, personalized marketing, or user tracking.

8. Data Retention

Saved articles and app preferences remain on the device until the user removes the app data or uninstalls the app.

Server-side news content is handled by the AI News Brief backend as needed for application operation and content management.

9. Data Deletion

AI News Brief does not require an account and does not maintain a user profile. Therefore, there is no account deletion process.

For locally stored app data, users can remove the data by uninstalling the app or using available device controls.

10. Children’s Privacy

AI News Brief is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration or user-submitted forms.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and its backend services. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Choices

You can:

  • Enable or disable Daily Brief
  • Manage notification permissions through iOS
  • Save or remove articles
  • Remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app
  • Choose whether to open original news articles

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, you can reach us through the contact information on the Support page.
